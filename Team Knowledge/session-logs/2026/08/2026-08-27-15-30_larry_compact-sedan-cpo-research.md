## Decisions made

- **Question:** Which budget/use-case frame drives the compact study?
  **Decision:** EV Household CPO Frame (Accord/Camry) — $20-25K, road-trip companion, TSP+ all years, highway range priority
- **Question:** Include hatchbacks?
  **Decision:** Yes — Civic Hatch, Corolla Hatch, Mazda3 Hatch, Impreza Hatch all included
- **Question:** Include Mazda3 Turbo AWD?
  **Decision:** No — over budget ($27-30K CPO)
- **Question:** Include Civic Hybrid?
  **Decision:** Future consideration only (2025+ too new for CPO at this price)
- **Question:** AWD requirement?
  **Decision:** Not required — FWD + winter tires acceptable per Accord study precedent

## Insights

- HondaTrue CPO warranty is uniquely strong in compact class: 2yr/100k comprehensive extension + $0 deductible + 2 free oil changes — no other brand extends bumper-to-bumper beyond 1yr/12k
- Hyundai/Kia 10yr/100k powertrain looks great on paper but $50 (Hyundai) / $0 (Kia) deductible + no comprehensive extension makes it weaker for actual ownership
- Nissan CPO has $100 deductible — only brand with triple-digit deductible in segment
- Civic Hatchback is the only compact with true flat-folding rear seats + hatch utility (24.5 cu ft) — Corolla Hatch doesn't fold flat, Mazda3 Hatch has less space (20.1 cu ft)
- Prius is priced out of compact CPO budget ($27-36K) — occupies no-man's-land between compact and midsize
- All viable compact options are FWD-only; Impreza AWD standard but 30 MPG misses efficiency target
