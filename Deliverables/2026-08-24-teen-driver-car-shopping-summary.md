## Top Recommendations

### 1. Honda Insight EX (2019-2020) — BEST OVERALL (Hybrid)

**Why it's #1:**
- ✅ **Only hybrid that hits all criteria AND fits $15K**
- 52 MPG combined — excellent fuel economy
- TSP+ highest safety rating
- Standard AEB + adaptive cruise on all trims
- Honda reliability — proven hybrid system
- 10-year/100K hybrid battery warranty

**Key details:**
- Must find **EX trim** (standard Honda Sensing)
- No AWD — FWD only
- 2019 models: $15K-$20K, 70K+ miles at $15K
- 2020 models: $18K-$22K

**Watch out for:**
- Discontinued — limited inventory
- 7 years old (2019) but Honda hybrid system is proven
- Verify hybrid battery health

---

### 2. Hyundai Elantra Hybrid SEL (2021) — BUDGET WINNER

**Why it's great:**
- ✅ **Only option that comfortably fits $15K budget**
- 54 MPG combined — best fuel economy in the group
- 10-year/100K powertrain warranty carries to second owner (huge for college)
- TSP safety rating, AEB standard

**Key details:**
- Must find **SEL trim or higher** — base Blue lacks adaptive cruise
- No AWD — FWD only

## Rejected Vehicles — Detailed Reasons

### Why Honda Civic Was Added (and Why It's Still Not Ideal)

The Honda Civic (2021-2022) was initially excluded because we focused on hybrids. After expanding to non-hybrids:

**Honda Civic LX (2021-2022):**
- ✅ TSP safety, 34 MPG combined / 40 MPG highway
- ✅ Standard AEB + adaptive cruise on all trims
- ❌ **PNW prices: $21K-$25K** — $6K+ over budget
- Verdict: Excellent car, but price rules it out at $15K

### Why Honda Insight Was Added Late

The Honda Insight (2019-2020) was initially excluded because we focused on 2021+ models. After expanding the year range:
- ✅ TSP+ safety, 52 MPG, standard AEB + ACC
- ✅ Fits $15K budget at 70K+ miles
- ⚠️ Discontinued, 7 years old (2019), limited inventory

### Hybrids Eliminated

**Toyota RAV4 Hybrid** — Over budget ($25K-$35K in PNW). Excellent vehicle but PNW Toyota tax makes it unaffordable at $15K.

**Honda Accord Hybrid** — Over budget ($23K-$29K) despite excellent 48 MPG and TSP+. Midsize sedan too expensive for this budget.

**Honda Civic Hybrid** — Not available in US until 2025. The US-market Civic Hybrid launched in 2025 only, making it too new and expensive.

**Hyundai Tucson Hybrid** — Over budget ($19K-$26K in PNW). Compact SUV with good features but PNW prices run high.

**Kia Sportage Hybrid** — Over budget ($20K-$27K). Shares platform with Tucson Hybrid, similar pricing issues.

**Subaru Forester Hybrid** — 2025+ only, $34K+ new, used still $24K+. Subaru's first hybrid SUV arrived too late and too expensive.

### Non-Hybrids Eliminated

**Volkswagen Jetta** — Failed multiple criteria: No hybrid option; 34 MPG; **no IIHS TSP** (Poor headlights, Poor crash tests); below average reliability; adaptive cruise requires expensive SEL trim. Fun to drive but fails safety and value criteria.

**Volkswagen Taos** — No hybrid; 31 MPG; **no IIHS TSP** (Marginal side test); over budget ($20K+). Subcompact SUV doesn't meet safety or price requirements.

**Volkswagen Tiguan** — No hybrid; 28 MPG; over budget ($20K-$30K); below average reliability. Compact SUV misses MPG target and costs too much.

**Chevrolet Malibu** — No hybrid (discontinued 2019); 32 MPG; **no IIHS TSP** (Poor side test, Poor headlights); AEB optional, not standard; average reliability. Midsize sedan fails safety criteria and lacks standard safety features.

**Chevrolet Equinox** — No hybrid; 26-28 MPG; over budget ($18K+); average reliability. Compact SUV misses MPG target significantly.

**Nissan Sentra** — 33 MPG (misses target); adaptive cruise requires SV trim; CVT concerns; Acceptable (not Good) side crash test. Budget sedan has transmission reliability questions.

**Kia Forte** — 2023+ has **Poor side test** — disqualifying for teen driver. Adaptive cruise limited to GT trim only. CVT issues reported. Budget sedan fails safety criteria for new driver.

**Ford Fusion** — Discontinued after 2020. No 2021+ models available. Was a good option but no longer in production.

**Ford Escape Hybrid** — 41 MPG but **failed TSP** (Marginal side test); over budget ($20K+). Good MPG but safety rating disqualifies it for teen driver.

**Ford Maverick Hybrid** — Truck body (not sedan/hatchback/SUV per criteria); 37 MPG (misses target); adaptive cruise only on $30K Lariat trim. Interesting vehicle but wrong category and price.

**Chevrolet Bolt EV** — Full EV, not hybrid; no adaptive cruise; battery recall concerns; requires charging infrastructure. Electric car doesn't meet hybrid requirement and has reliability history.

### Subaru Eliminated

**Subaru Crosstrek (non-hybrid)** — 29 MPG — 11 MPG below target. Great AWD system but fuel economy disqualifies it.

**Subaru Forester (non-hybrid)** — 29 MPG; no hybrid until 2025+; over budget. Compact SUV misses MPG target significantly.

**Subaru Outback** — 28 MPG; no hybrid; over budget ($18K+); highest NHTSA complaint volume. Wagon/SUV misses MPG target and has reliability concerns.

**Subaru Legacy** — 27 MPG; no hybrid; discontinued. Sedan discontinued, misses MPG target.

**Subaru Solterra** — Full EV, not hybrid; $22K+; lowest Subaru reliability rating. Electric SUV has reliability concerns and doesn't meet hybrid requirement.

## Vancouver, WA Tax Note

Washington residents pay **9.4% use tax** on any vehicle purchase, whether bought in Oregon or Washington. Buying in Oregon provides **no tax advantage**.

- **Tax on $15K car:** ~$1,410
- **Tax on $20K car:** ~$1,880

**Bottom line:** Focus on finding the lowest price locally; tax is the same everywhere.

## Pre-Purchase Checklist

Before buying any used car:

- [ ] Verify IIHS rating for specific model year at IIHS.org
- [ ] Confirm AEB and adaptive cruise are standard or on the specific trim
- [ ] Check NHTSA recalls at NHTSA.gov and verify completion
- [ ] Get vehicle history report (Carfax/AutoCheck)
- [ ] Verify powertrain warranty transferability (Hyundai/Kia 10yr/100K)
- [ ] Test drive — check visibility, controls, parking ease for new driver
- [ ] Get insurance quote before final decision
- [ ] Pre-purchase inspection by independent mechanic
- [ ] For hybrids: check battery health and warranty status
