## Executive Summary

**No Subaru model simultaneously meets all eight criteria.** The core tension: Subaru's best hybrids (Crosstrek Hybrid, Forester Hybrid) achieve 35 MPG — still 5 MPG short of the ~40 MPG target — and the only Subaru hybrid that fits the $15K budget is a used 2021 Crosstrek Hybrid (PHEV), which is scarce in PNW inventory. The **2021-2023 Crosstrek Hybrid** comes closest to checking all boxes. The **2024+ Crosstrek** (non-hybrid) is the strongest overall value pick if the MPG target is relaxed to 29 MPG.

## Subaru-Specific Issues to Know

### CVT Transmission
- **2012-2017 models:** Highest complaint volume. Shuddering, hesitation, and failures reported. Subaru extended CVT warranty to 10yr/100K miles for affected vehicles.
- **2018+ models:** Significantly improved. Stronger chain, updated valve body, better fluid. Still requires fluid changes every 30-60K miles (Subaru's old "lifetime fill" guidance was a mistake).
- **2020-2021 turbo Outback/Legacy:** Specific CVT chain slip recall (WRK-21/22). Verify recall completion before purchase.
- **Recommendation:** For a teen driver, prioritize 2018+ models. Verify CVT fluid has been changed at appropriate intervals.

### Oil Consumption
- **2012-2014 models:** Worst offenders. Some engines burn 1+ quart per 1,000 miles.
- **2015+ models:** Substantially improved. Consumer Reports shows near-zero reported oil consumption issues in 2019+ models.
- **Boxer engine design:** All Subaru boxer engines consume slightly more oil than inline engines by design. Up to 1 quart per 3,000-4,000 miles is considered normal.
- **Recommendation:** Avoid 2012-2014 models. 2019+ models are reliable in this regard.

### Direct Injection Carbon Buildup
- All 2018+ Subaru engines use direct injection. Carbon buildup on intake valves is an inherent DI issue.
- Symptoms appear around 80K-100K miles (rough idle, reduced performance).
- Fix: Walnut blasting (~$300-$500). Not urgent for a teen driver who will likely replace the car before 100K miles.
- **Mitigation:** Use quality fuel, avoid excessive short trips.

### Head Gaskets
- The old EJ engine head gasket problem was resolved with the FB/FA engine redesign (2010+). Not a concern for 2021+ models.

## Recommendations

### If the MPG target is firm at ~40 MPG:
**No Subaru qualifies.** The best Subaru hybrid gets 35 MPG. Consider:
- **Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid** (42 MPG combined, TSP, AWD, ~$22K used)
- **Toyota RAV4 Hybrid** (40 MPG combined, TSP+, AWD, ~$25K used)
- **Honda CR-V Hybrid** (35-40 MPG depending on year, TSP+, AWD)

### If the MPG target can flex to 30-35 MPG:
1. **Best overall pick: 2021-2023 Crosstrek Hybrid (PHEV)** — 35 MPG, TSP+, $16K-$21K, EyeSight standard. Search for one with documented maintenance and recall completion.
2. **Best value pick: 2021-2022 Impreza** — 30 MPG, $15K-$20K, best Subaru reliability scores. No hybrid, but cheapest entry.
3. **Budget pick: 2021 Crosstrek 2.5** — 29 MPG, $17K-$22K, TSP with EyeSight. The most practical "good enough" option.

### Pre-Purchase Checklist (PNW-specific):
1. **Verify CVT recall completion** (WRK-21/22 for 2019-2021 turbo models)
2. **Request CVT fluid change history** — should be done every 30-60K miles
3. **Check for oil consumption** — request oil change records
4. **Get a pre-purchase inspection** — Subaru dealerships in Portland: Subaru of Portland, Lithia Subaru of Oregon City, Royal Moore Subaru (Hillsboro)
5. **Check Carfax/Autocheck** for accident history — important for a teen driver's first car
6. **For PHEV:** Verify charging port works, check battery health, confirm Level 2 charging capability
