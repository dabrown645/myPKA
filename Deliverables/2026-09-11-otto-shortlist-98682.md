## OTD math (98682 Vancouver WA)
- 8.9% sales tax, WA doc max $200 (negotiable, not taxed), ~$300 title/reg
- OTD ≈ selling price × 1.089 + $500 → $35,000 OTD = **$31,680 selling-price ceiling**
- No trade-in assumed. Targets below are asking price before negotiation.

## Gates applied
1. Top safety: IIHS TSP/TSP+ year + standard AEB + blind-spot. 2024+ preferred post-update.
2. Heated front seats standard on the trim (not optional package hunt unless confirmed present).
3. CPO 2022+, <40k miles preferred; 43-45k tolerated only for Toyota with records.
4. 5-passenger road-trip fit for 4: cargo, rear legroom, noise, mpg.

## Ranked picks

### 1. CPO 2024 Kia Sportage Hybrid EX AWD — Otto's pick
- Why: 2024 earned TSP+ after standard LED projection fix (2023 was TSP only on top trims due to poor base headlights). EX has heated front bucket seats confirmed, 38mpg, ~73.7 cu ft max, 10yr/100k powertrain + 12mo/12k Platinum CPO, $50 deductible.
- Market: CPO EX AWD examples $26–29k at 12–36k miles — OTD $28.8–32.1k at 98682. Comfortable headroom under cap.
- Target: 2024 EX AWD, <35k miles, ≤$29,500 asking → ~$32.6k OTD.
- Watch: 6-speed hesitation on sudden gaps; verify build date and headlight trim; confirm heated seats on the specific VIN (LX lacks them).

### 2. CPO 2024 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid SEL Convenience AWD
- Why: same platform as Sportage, TSP+ lineage (2022 TSP+), reinforced B-pillar + rear side thorax airbag from 2024, 74.8 cu ft max, 2,750-lb gas tow best-in-class if utility matters. SEL Convenience adds heated seats + 12.3-in tech.
- Market: mirrors Sportage, expect mid-to-high $20s CPO. Same 10yr/100k powertrain story.
- Target: 2024 SEL Convenience, <35k miles, ≤$29,500 asking.
- Watch: AWD hybrid ~37–38mpg combined, a touch behind RAV4; confirm March-2024+ build for the rear-pretensioner update.

### 3. CPO 2022–2023 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid XLE AWD *with Weather Package* — stretch pick
- Why: efficiency/resale champ (40–41mpg), Toyota Safety Sense standard, 2022 TSP / 2023 TSP+. Best long-hold story for a retiree keeping 8–10 years.
- Catch: heated seats are NOT standard on XLE — only with XLE Grade Weather Package. Many listings lack it; filter hard.
- Market: 2022 XLE $30,994/43k mi → ~$34.3k OTD fits. 2023 $31,292/43k mi → ~$34.6k OTD fits. 2024 $36k+ busts. Miles run higher than Korean picks at the same money.
- Target: 2022–2023 XLE + Weather + Convenience, ≤45k miles, 1-owner, ≤$31,500 asking. Walk if no Weather Package or >45k miles.
- Watch: stiffer ride + highway wind noise vs CR-V; side updated-test rear head protection Marginal — still strong overall but know the footnote.
