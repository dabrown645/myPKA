## Executive summary

Top 2 luxury to final 5:

1. **Lexus ES 300h — 8/10.** 43/44/44 x 13.2 = 581mi PASS, regular gas, EPA Midsize, L/Certified 2yr/unlim $0 + 10/150k battery. Most efficient non-plug-in luxury sedan.
2. **Genesis G80 2.5T AWD — 7/10.** TSP+ 2024 (post-Oct 2023) + 2026 TSP+ (post-June 2025), 20/29/24 x 17.3 = 502mi borderline PASS, 300hp, 10/100k PT best warranty. Value vs Germans.

Park: E350/530i (comfort/driver but $$ + TSP-only). Eliminate on bar: A6/A7 (no TSP), S90 (493mi FAIL), CT5 (no TSP, least luxe base), XF (498mi FAIL, network risk), ES 350 gas as ESh backup only.

## Key findings

1. **ES 300h only clean luxury fit — High.** 215hp hybrid eCVT FWD, 97 cu ft pass / 14 trunk, heated/BSM/ACC expected std (verify F Sport vs base). 2 recalls (check VIN). PNW CPO $32-42k.
2. **G80 best warranty + presence — High.** 2.5T 300hp/311lb-ft 8AT AWD, 42.2/38.7 legroom, 13.1 trunk. 2025 refresh 27" OLED. 3.5T 5.0s but FAIL range. CPO: 5/60k basic remainder + 10/100k PT. $57-78k new → $32-44k CPO.
3. **E350 E-Class / 530i 5-Series — Medium-High.** E350 24/33 x ~17.4 = ~574mi, 2024 TSP; 530i 27/35 x ~18 = ~630mi, 2024 TSP. Premium fuel, 1yr/unlim CPO ext after 4/50k, maintenance $$ ($1,500-2,500/yr post-warranty per CR/Edmunds logic). Comfort (E) vs driver (5).
4. **A6/A7 — Medium.** A6 2.0 23/33 x 19.3 = 636mi PASS premium, 3.0 22/30. CR: best-tested manners, firm ride, 26mpg observed, 1 recall. No TSP 24/25 in pulls. A7 hatch utility same powertrain.
5. **S90 / CT5 / XF out — Medium-High.** S90 B6 22/31 x 15.9 = 493mi FAIL, T8 PHEV fixes but $$; Volvo CPO 12mo/unlim $0. CT5 2.0 23/33 x 17.2 = 568mi PASS, RWD-biased, Super Cruise option, no TSP, 1yr/unlim CPO. XF P250 23/30 x 16.6 = 498mi FAIL, no TSP, Jaguar ICE wind-down.
