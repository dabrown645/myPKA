## Key findings

1. **Existence — High.** Malibu 2023/24/25 only. Fusion out July 2020 (out of 1-3yr window), 300/Charger full-size, Regal/MKZ out 2020. No other domestic midsize to score.
2. **Range PASS — High.** 1.5T 27/35/31 x 15.8 = 553mi PASS. No hybrid (dropped 2020). CVT only.
3. **Safety FAIL bar — High.** No TSP/TSP+ in IIHS 2024 or 2025 midsize lists (winners: Accord/Camry/Sonata only). CR Safety Verdict: Basic. C/D 6/10 "nowhere near as well-rounded." FCW/AEB std 2023+, BSM/RCTA optional, rear park std 2024.
4. **Space/comfort — Medium-High.** 38.1" rear, 15.7 cu ft trunk, quiet cabin, comfortable ride. Heated: LT recommended (LS lacks, Edmunds pick LT).
5. **Reliability/CPO — Medium.** RepairPal 4/5 Excellent #10/28 midsize, 11% severe rate. Chevy CPO: remainder 3/36 + 6/100k PT? — verify booklet; 1yr/unlim CPO extension typical, $0-$50 ded by dealer. Heavy fleet mix (50-75% 2023 per Cox) — check ex-rental history.
6. **PNW $ — Medium.** New $25-32k, CPO $18-25k — cheapest in final 5 by $5-10k. Insurance ~$1,600, fuel $1,700/yr @15k (31 combined).
