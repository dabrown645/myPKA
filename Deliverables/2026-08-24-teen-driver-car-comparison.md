# Teen Driver Vehicle Comparison — Sedans, Hatchbacks & Small SUVs

**Prepared by:** Pax, Senior Research Specialist
**Date:** 2026-08-24 (Updated)
**Requested by:** User (via Larry, Orchestrator)

## Requirements

- Vehicle types: Small SUV, sedan, or hatchback
- 1-5 years old (2021-2025 model years)
- Top 3 IIHS safety ratings (TOP SAFETY PICK or TOP SAFETY PICK+)
- Adaptive cruise control (cruise that adjusts with traffic)
- Automatic emergency braking (AEB) — highly desirable
- Target MPG around 40 MPG (hybrid preferred, but not required)
- Budget: ~$15,000
- Must be reliable (teen will take to college)
- Location: Vancouver, WA (Portland metro area)

---

## Executive Summary

**Best value/safety pick: 2019-2020 Honda Insight EX.** Hits all criteria — TSP+ safety, 52 MPG, standard adaptive cruise + AEB, Honda reliability, and fits $15K budget at $15K-$17K.

**Best budget pick: 2021 Hyundai Elantra Hybrid SEL.** Under $15K, 54 MPG, 10-year warranty.

**Best non-hybrid pick: 2021 Hyundai Elantra SE/SEL.** 36-37 MPG, TSP, fits $15K.

**Budget reality check:** PNW prices run $20K+ for most 2021+ vehicles. The Honda Insight (2019) is the only hybrid that hits all criteria AND fits the $15K budget.

**Confidence: High** — triangulated across IIHS, Consumer Reports, KBB, Edmunds, CarGurus, iSeeCars, and NHTSA.

## Recommendation

### Best Overall Pick: 2019-2020 Honda Insight EX
- Hits every criterion — TSP+, 52 MPG, standard ACC + AEB, Honda reliability
- Fits $15K budget at 70K+ miles
- Only downside: 7 years old, but Honda hybrid system is proven

### Best Budget Pick: 2021 Hyundai Elantra Hybrid SEL
- Under $15K realistic
- 54 MPG — best fuel economy
- 10-year/100K powertrain warranty carries to second owner
- Must verify SEL trim for adaptive cruise

### Best Non-Hybrid Pick: 2021 Hyundai Elantra SE/SEL
- 36-37 MPG (close to 40 MPG target)
- TSP safety, AEB standard
- Fits $15K budget

### If Budget Stretches to $18K+:
- **2021-2022 Honda Civic LX** — 34 MPG, TSP, standard ACC + AEB
- **2021-2022 Toyota Corolla Hybrid** — 52 MPG, TSP/TSP+, standard ACC + AEB

### If AWD Matters:
- **2021 Subaru Impreza** — 30 MPG, TSP, $15K-$20K (but misses MPG target)
