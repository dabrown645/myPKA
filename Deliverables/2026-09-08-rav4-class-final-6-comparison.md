## Executive summary

Top 2 from each group, measured against the project's 10-point acceptance criteria:

1. **Toyota RAV4 Hybrid — 9/10.** 551mi AWD, $0 CPO, 10yr/150k battery. Only miss is TSP (not TSP+).
2. **Hyundai Tucson Hybrid Blue — 8/10.** TSP+ all years, 521mi, 38.7 cu ft / 41.3" best space, ~$25.5k avg. Blue-trim-only range, CR 2023 below avg, $50 ded.
3. **Volvo XC60 B5 — 8/10.** 526-564mi every trim, $0 unlimited CPO. TSP only, 21.6 cu ft smallest, $$, camera recall, ACC Plus+ only.
4. **Lexus NX 350h (2024-25) — 7/10.** TSP+, 537mi, 2yr/unlimited $0 + 10/150k battery. 22.7 cu ft / 36.1" smallest cabin, $41-53k.
5. **Ford Escape Hybrid — 6/10.** 515mi, 34.4 cu ft, 7yr/100k $0. No TSP any year, heavy recalls (1.5L cylinder-head fire, PHEV fire-sibling, EGR), heated ST-Line Select only.
6. **VW Tiguan 2023-24 — 6/10.** TSP+, std heated/ACC/BS, $22-33k. 459-461mi FAIL, 2yr/24k $50 short powertrain, oil-consumption watch. 2025 fails TSP (Poor mod overlap).

If range is the veto: RAV4 vs Tucson Blue vs XC60. If TSP+ is the veto: Tucson Blue vs NX 350h.

## Per-vehicle notes

### 1. Toyota RAV4 Hybrid AWD (2.5L HEV, 38mpg x 14.5gal = 551mi)
Heated gate: skip LE entirely (no heat, no BSM). Minimum XLE with Weather Package on sticker; XSE/Limited to guarantee. TSS 2.5 ACC all trims; BSM XLE+. CR much more reliable 2025. Best battery warranty. PNW $30-37k.

### 2. Hyundai Tucson Hybrid Blue (1.6T HEV, 38mpg x 13.7gal = 521mi)
Only Blue clears range — SEL/Limited 36mpg = 493 FAIL. Any trim clears heat; Limited adds ventilated + heated rear/wheel. Full SmartSense std on Blue; Limited adds BVM/HDA/surround. TSP+ all years (24 post-Mar build). CR 2023 below avg is the flag. $50 ded. PNW Blue thin — verify trim on lot.

### 3. Volvo XC60 B5 (mild hybrid, 28-30mpg x 18.8gal = 526-564mi)
Only entry to clear range in every trim (B6 508, T8 total 560). TSP only (side Acceptable, night ped Advanced). ACC Pilot Assist Plus+ only — not base Core. Cargo 21.6 is the penalty. CR above avg 2023. 12mo/unlimited $0, upgradeable to 5yr. Camera recall 25V282000 (413k units). $36-45k.

### 4. Lexus NX 350h (2.5L HEV, 37mpg x 14.5gal = 537mi)
NX 250 gas 479/464 FAIL — hybrid only. TSP+ 24-25 (23 TSP). Heated/ACC/BS all trims. 22.7/46.9 cargo and 36.1" rear are smallest. JD segment winner; CR avg 2024. L/Certified 2yr/unlimited $0 + maint, battery 10/150k. $41-53k. 450h+ PHEV: 479 gas / 516 total.

### 5. Ford Escape Hybrid (2.5L HEV, 36mpg x 14.3gal = 515mi)
Gas 1.5L also passes (503-534mi). No TSP any year (Marginal side + Marginal FCP). Heated ST-Line Select only; BS all, ACC higher trims. Gas space 37.5/40.7 best domestic; hybrid 34.4/38.9. CR less 2024. Ford Blue Gold 12mo/12k + 7yr/100k $0 (best duration). Heavy recalls: 1.5L cylinder-head oil/fire 23-25, PHEV cell fire (sibling), EGR, heater. $20-35k.

### 6. VW Tiguan 2023-24 (2.0T, 30/29mpg x 15.3/15.9gal = 459-461mi FAIL)
TSP+ 23-24; 2025 FAILs TSP (Poor updated mod overlap). Heated/ACC/BS all std (check 23-24 chip-delete stickers). 2025 S 34mpg = 493mi still FAIL. CR less 23 / more 24; iSeeCars 7.9 best domestic-group score but oil-consumption complaints. VW CPO 2yr/24k $50 — longest comprehensive, shortest powertrain. $22-33k.
