1. **Honda Accord Hybrid EX-L — 9/10.** TSP+ all years, 563-614mi, 40.8" rear / 16.7 trunk best, HondaTrue $0. The buy if NVH + rear comfort + warranty rule.

5. **Chevrolet Malibu LT — 6/10.** 553mi PASS, $18-25k cheapest, quiet/roomy. No TSP — price anchor only, expect eliminate on safety bar.

If range is veto: Accord vs Camry vs ES. If TSP+ is veto: Accord vs Camry vs G80 (gated). If budget is veto: Malibu only.

## Per-vehicle notes

### 1. Accord Hybrid EX-L (2.0 HEV, 51/44/48, 12.8gal)
Minimum EX-L for leather/heat/BSM std. 11th-gen TSP+ structure. Best NVH + rear for 400mi days. HondaTrue extends factory — warranty you'll actually use. PNW thin EX-L — Touring fallback $31-33k.

### 2. Lexus ES 300h (2.5 HEV, 43/44/44, 13.2gal)
215hp eCVT, most efficient non-plug-in luxury sedan. Regular gas (vs premium Germans). 2 recalls — VIN check. Verify TSP plate + heated/BSM (F Sport vs base split). Best long-hold bet.

### 3. Camry Hybrid LE/XLE (2.5 HEV, 51/53/52 LE, 13.2gal)
208hp THS II. LE = range max but no BSM — XLE minimum for your ADAS gate (+leather, -6mpg). 2025+ all-hybrid (no gas) — future-proofs CPO pool. Smallest trunk, seats not flat.

### 4. G80 2.5T (2.5T 300hp, 20/29/24, 17.3gal, AWD std)
95% of E/5 for $10k less CPO. 27" OLED 2025 refresh. Verify Oct 2023 / June 2025 build for TSP+. Premium fuel + 24 combined hurt running cost. 3.5T fails range — 2.5T only.

### 5. Malibu LT (1.5T 163hp CVT, 27/35/31, 15.8gal)
Price anchor. Quiet, roomy, LT heat. No TSP, fleet history risk, 2016 platform (oldest). Buy only if $8k+ under Accord/Camry OTD + winter package + 1yr CPO in writing.
