# EV Household Second Car: Non-SUV Sedan Comparison (2022-2024)
**Prepared by Pax, Researcher | For Vancouver, WA / Portland, OR Metro | August 2025**

---

## Executive Summary

**Top Recommendation: 2023-2024 Honda Accord Hybrid EX-L (CPO)**

Best overall balance of safety (TSP+ all years), highway comfort, reliability, cargo space, and CPO value in the $25-30K range. The Accord Hybrid's superior NVH, spacious rear seat, and HondaTrue Certified warranty (7yr/100k powertrain + extended non-powertrain) make it the ideal road-trip companion to an EV primary.

**Runner-Up: 2023-2024 Toyota Camry Hybrid LE/XLE (CPO)**

Slightly better highway MPG (53 vs 44) and legendary Toyota hybrid reliability, but smaller trunk (15.1 vs 16.7 cu ft), no rear seat fold-flat, and shorter CPO comprehensive warranty (12mo/12k vs Honda's 2yr extension).

**Budget Pick: 2023-2024 Hyundai Sonata Hybrid SEL (CPO)**

Best highway range (~811 mi), lowest CPO pricing ($22-25K), and 10yr/100k powertrain warranty — but only TSP (not TSP+), firmer ride, and $50 deductible on powertrain claims.

**Eliminate: Kia K5 (gas only, 31 MPG combined) — fails MPG requirement.**

**Niche Option: 2023-2024 Toyota Prius (hatchback)**

Incredible MPG (56-57 combined) and TSP+, but compromised rear seat comfort, lower roofline, and smaller highway range (~633 mi) due to 11.3 gal tank. Only consider if cargo versatility (hatch + fold-flat seats) outweighs passenger comfort.

## Deep Dive: Each Viable Option

### 1. Honda Accord Hybrid (2023-2024) — **TOP PICK**

**Why it wins for EV household road trips:**
- **Only sedan with TSP+ across all three model years** (2022, 2023, 2024)
- **Best highway NVH and seat comfort** — critical for 400+ mile days
- **Largest rear legroom (40.8")** — passengers actually comfortable on long trips
- **HondaTrue CPO warranty** effectively extends bumper-to-bumper to 4yr/48k from original sale (or adds 1yr/12k from purchase) + 7yr/100k powertrain
- **Standard blind-spot monitoring** on all hybrid trims (Toyota charges extra on base)
- **14.8 gal tank × 44 MPG highway = 651 mi real-world range** — sufficient for I-5 Portland→Seattle→Portland round trip (350 mi) with reserve

**CPO Sweet Spot:** 2023 EX-L or Sport-L with 20-35K miles (~$28-30K). Gets you the 11th-gen redesign (better side impact structure = TSP+), 48 MPG combined, and ~2+ years of factory bumper-to-bumper remaining.

**PNW Dealer Presence:** Ron Tonkin Honda (Portland), Beaverton Honda, Dick Hannah Honda (Vancouver) — all major CPO volume dealers.

---

### 2. Toyota Camry Hybrid (2022-2024) — **RUNNER-UP**

**Strengths:**
- **Best highway MPG of the sedans (53 LE trim)** → 837 mi theoretical range
- **Toyota hybrid reliability is the industry benchmark** — 200k+ miles common
- **TSP+ for 2023-2024** (2022 is TSP only)
- **Lower CPO pricing** than Accord for equivalent age/mileage

**Weaknesses for road trips:**
- **Smallest trunk (15.1 cu ft)** and rear seats don't fold flat — limits luggage/gear
- **CPO comprehensive warranty only 12mo/12k** (vs Honda's 2yr extension)
- **Blind-spot monitoring optional on LE/SE** (standard on Honda hybrids)
- **No AWD ever** on this generation
- **Stiffer rear suspension** — less compliant on long highway stretches

**CPO Sweet Spot:** 2023 LE or XLE with 30-45K miles (~$25-27K). LE gets 53 MPG highway; XLE adds leather, power seats, but drops to 47 MPG highway.

---

### 3. Hyundai Sonata Hybrid (2022-2024) — **BUDGET VALUE PICK**

**Strengths:**
- **Best highway range** (Blue trim: 54 MPG × 15.9 gal = 859 mi)
- **Lowest CPO pricing** — 2023 SEL CPO ~$22-25K leaves budget for winter tires + roof rack
- **10yr/100k powertrain warranty from original in-service date** (longest coverage)
- **Standard blind-spot, rear cross-traffic, safe exit assist** — great safety tech value
- **Spacious interior** despite fastback roofline

**Critical Weakness:**
- **Only TSP (not TSP+)** due to **Poor rating in updated moderate overlap front test** (driver chest protection)
- **$50 deductible per powertrain warranty visit** (Toyota/Honda = $0)
- **Firmer ride** — more road noise on coarse PNW highways
- **Hyundai CPO adds NO bumper-to-bumper extension** — only remainder of 5yr/60k factory

**CPO Sweet Spot:** 2023 SEL with 35-45K miles (~$23-24K). Blue trim (52 MPG combined) discontinued for 2024.

---

### 4. Toyota Prius 5th Gen (2023-2024) — **NICHE: CARGO OVER COMFORT**

**Only consider if:** You regularly haul bulky items (bikes, large luggage, Costco runs) and can accept compromised rear passenger space.

**Pros:**
- **Best combined MPG (57 LE)** — lowest fuel cost per mile
- **TSP+ (2023-2024)**
- **Hatchback + fold-flat seats = 20.3 → 50+ cu ft cargo versatility**
- **AWD-e available (2023+)** — modest snow help
- **Toyota Gold CPO warranty**

**Cons for road trips:**
- **11.3 gal tank limits highway range to ~633 mi** — more stops on I-5
- **Rear seat cramped** (35.6" legroom, low roofline) — adults unhappy >2 hrs
- **Lower seating position** — harder entry/exit, less commanding view
- **CPO pricing near top of budget** ($27-30K for 2023-2024 LE)

## CPO Warranty Comparison (Critical for 1-3 Year Old Vehicles)

| Feature | **Toyota Gold** | **HondaTrue** | **Hyundai CPO** |
|---------|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Comprehensive/Bumper-to-Bumper** | 12mo/12k from purchase | Extends factory to 4yr/48k OR adds 1yr/12k from purchase | **None** (only remainder of 5yr/60k factory) |
| **Powertrain** | 7yr/100k from original in-service | 7yr/100k from original in-service | **10yr/100k from original in-service** |
| **Deductible** | $0 | $0 | **$50 per visit** |
| **Roadside Assistance** | 7yr/100k | Duration of warranty | 10yr/unlimited miles |
| **Transferable** | Yes, no cost | Yes | Yes |
| **Trip Interruption** | $100/day × 5 days | Yes | Yes (rental $35/day × 10 days) |
| **Hybrid Battery** | 10yr/150k from original (2020+) | 8yr/100k from original | 10yr/100k from original |

**Key Insight:** For a 2023 model bought in 2025:
- **Toyota:** 12mo/12k comprehensive + ~5yr/60k powertrain remaining
- **Honda:** ~2yr/24k comprehensive (extends factory) + ~5yr/60k powertrain remaining
- **Hyundai:** ~2yr/15k comprehensive remaining + ~8yr/60k powertrain remaining (with $50 deductible)

**Honda wins on comprehensive coverage duration** — the warranty you'll actually use for electronics, A/C, suspension, etc.

## PNW-Specific Considerations

### FWD Only + Vancouver/PDX Winter
- **All four are FWD-only** (Prius AWD-e is not true AWD)
- **Action item:** Budget $800-1,200 for dedicated winter tires (Michelin X-Ice, Bridgestone Blizzak) on separate wheels
- **Ground clearance:** All ~5.5-6.0" — adequate for plowed I-5/US-26, not for unplowed mountain passes
- **Heated seats/steering:** Standard on Accord Hybrid EX-L+, Camry XLE+, Sonata Limited, Prius XLE+

### Road Trip Routes from PNW

## Recommendation Logic

### Primary: 2023-2024 Honda Accord Hybrid EX-L CPO
```
Budget: $28,000-30,000
Mileage: 20,000-35,000
Trim: EX-L (51/44/48 MPG, leather, heated seats, BSM standard, HUD avail on Touring)
Warranty: ~2.5 yr factory bumper-to-bumper + 7yr/100k powertrain
Safety: TSP+ (all years)
Comfort: Best-in-class NVH, rear seat space, seat comfort
Dealer: Ron Tonkin Honda, Beaverton Honda, Dick Hannah Honda
```

### Alternative if Accord unavailable: 2023 Toyota Camry Hybrid XLE CPO
```
Budget: $26,000-28,000
Mileage: 30,000-45,000
Trim: XLE (44/47/46 MPG, leather, power seats, BSM standard)
Warranty: 12mo/12k comprehensive + ~5yr powertrain
Safety: TSP+ (2023 post-Jan build)
Trade-off: Less trunk, less rear room, shorter comprehensive warranty
```

### Budget Stretch: 2023 Hyundai Sonata Hybrid SEL CPO + Winter Tires
```
Budget: $23,000 (car) + $1,000 (winter tires/wheels) = $24,000 total
Mileage: 35,000-45,000
Trim: SEL (44/51/47 MPG, BSM standard, heated seats, 10.25" screen)
Warranty: 10yr/100k powertrain ($50 deductible) + ~2yr factory bumper-to-bumper
Safety: TSP only (Poor updated moderate overlap front)
Acceptable if: Budget is hard constraint, max highway range priority
```
