# Passport

> [!example] Seeded course sample
> This is a worked example the myICOR myPKA course walks through — it shows the canonical shape to follow. Keep it as a reference and adapt it, or replace its contents with your own.

## Summary

Government-issued identity document. The scanned PDF is stored in the encrypted vault under `documents/identity/`; a photo of the data page is also in my password manager's secure notes.

## Key terms

Expiry: check the data page. The renewal trigger fires nine months before expiry, because some destinations refuse entry if the passport expires within six months of arrival.

- nine months before expiry, start the renewal
- six months before expiry, renewal must be submitted
- if I'm planning international travel, check expiry before booking, every time

## Notes

Keep the old passport after renewal. Some visa records sit inside it and may be needed later.
