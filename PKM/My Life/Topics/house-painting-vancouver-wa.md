## Tips for Hiring
- Get **at least 3 written estimates** before deciding
- Ask about **prep work** (pressure washing, scraping, priming) — this matters more than the paint itself
- Verify **license and insurance** before work starts
- Ask what **brand and grade of paint** they use (e.g., Sherwin-Williams Duration, Benjamin Moore Aura)
- Get **warranty details** on labor (typical: 1-3 years)
- Check **recent Google reviews** — look for exterior painting photos specifically
- Typical exterior cost in Vancouver WA: single-story $2,500-5,500, two-story $4,000-8,000+
