# AI-enhanced journaling

## What I think about here
The concept of using AI to transform journaling from a simple recording activity into an active decision-making system. Rather than just storing thoughts, AI-enhanced journaling aims to make journal entries actionable through proper structure, tagging, and automated processing that surfaces relevant information when needed.

Key aspects include:
- Structured tagging systems (like the Interaction/Note/Thought/Milestone framework)
- Key element/topic classification for organization
- Automatic linking and surfacing of related entries
- Pattern recognition across entries over time
- Specialized intelligence processing for different content types

## Open questions
- How does this approach integrate with or differ from existing myPKA entity systems?
- What specific tagging frameworks work best for different types of content?
- How can we balance structure with the natural flow of thought in journaling?
- What role should AI play vs. human judgment in processing journal entries?
