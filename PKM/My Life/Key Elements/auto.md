# Auto

## What this covers

Second car research and ownership, EV household setup, road-trip capability, CPO buying, maintenance and insurance. Projects like [[car-research-for-second-car]] belong here.

## What good looks like

Reliable two-car setup where the EV covers daily use and the second car covers road trips and backup commuting without surprises — right CPO warranty in writing, known OTD cost, winter-ready.

## What I am ignoring

Teen-driver training specifics live with that project once split out. This element does not track fuel logs or maintenance streaks — those are Habit-level if needed.
