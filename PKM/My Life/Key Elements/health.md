# Health

> [!example] Seeded course sample
> This is a worked example the myICOR myPKA course walks through — it shows the canonical shape to follow. Keep it as a reference and adapt it, or replace its contents with your own.

## What this covers

A dimension of my life, not a goal. Health is something I tend to, not something I finish. This note exists to keep that distinction clear when I'm setting Goals or running Habits.

Health here is broader than the medical version. It includes physical, mental, creative, and relational well-being. If any one of those is starving, the others start to leak. That breadth is why [[ship-mvp-by-q3]] sits inside this Key Element, and why [[morning-build-session]] anchors here - creative and mental health show up in that block first.

## What good looks like

Signals I pay attention to:

- energy at the start of the [[morning-build-session]] - if I'm dragging on day one of the week, something upstream is off
- whether I'm building things I care about
- recovery quality, not just sleep hours
- how often I say yes to things that don't feed any of the four dimensions

## What I am ignoring

This Key Element is not a tracker, a streak, or a number to optimize. It's the lens I use when I'm deciding whether a Goal or Project belongs in my life right now.
