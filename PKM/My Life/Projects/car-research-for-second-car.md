# Car research for second car

## Why this matters

EV is primary. This second car covers road trips where charging is inconvenient and occasional commuting. CPO 1-3 years old with warranty, PNW pricing, heated-seats requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

From August EV-household studies — must meet all to buy:

- [ ] CPO 1-3 years old, warranty in writing (HondaTrue 2yr comprehensive $0 ded. preferred; Toyota 12mo/12k; Hyundai $50 ded. noted)
- [ ] TSP+ preferred, TSP minimum — no Poor updated-overlap scores
- [ ] Highway range 500mi+ (tank x hwy MPG, PNW Seattle / Bend / Boise / Redwoods legs)
- [ ] Heated seats minimum (household gate — eliminates base Sport trims)
  - RAV4 Hybrid US: LE has NO heat — minimum XLE + Weather Package on sticker, or XSE/Limited to guarantee. Limited adds ventilated + avail heated rear.
  - Tucson Hybrid US: any trim clears heat — Blue base heated front std. Limited adds ventilated front + heated rear + heated wheel.
- [ ] Adaptive cruise + blind-spot standard on trim bought
  - RAV4 US: ACC all trims (TSS 2.5), but BSM/RCTA XLE and up only — LE has no BSM. Higher trims add parking sensors/surround-view.
  - Tucson US: ACC + BSM + RCCA all trims std. Limited only adds BSM camera, HDA lane-centering, surround-view, park sensors.
- [ ] Rear legroom and NVH OK for 400+ mile days, cargo fits road-trip load (flat-fold preferred for hatch)
- [ ] FWD + winter tires acceptable; AWD bonus only
- [ ] PNW inventory: Vancouver WA / Portland metro, verified CPO listing
- [ ] Reliability average or better, hybrid battery warranty confirmed
- [ ] OTD with winter tire package within stretch budget, insurance quoted
