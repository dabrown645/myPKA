# Morning Build Session

> [!example] Seeded course sample
> This is a worked example the myICOR myPKA course walks through — it shows the canonical shape to follow. Keep it as a reference and adapt it, or replace its contents with your own.

## Why this habit

The first focused block of the day is the most honest one. No meetings have eroded it, no inbox has hijacked it. If I spend it building, the rest of the day stays pulled forward instead of pushed around.

## What it looks like

Weekdays, 90 minutes, starting within 30 minutes of sitting down at the desk. What counts as done:

- one concrete artifact moved forward (a commit, a doc, a decision written down)
- no email, no Slack, no chat tools open during the block
- a one-line note at the end of the block answering "what got moved"

Yardstick: at week's end, I should be able to name five artifacts the morning sessions produced. Three is acceptable. Two means the habit slipped and needs a reset.

## Reflection

This block is where the MVP gets built: it supports [[ship-mvp-by-q3]] directly, feeds [[side-project-mvp]] when the day's session is pointed at it (the Project is that Goal's carrier), and ties into [[ai-tooling]] when I use the block to test or refine a tooling workflow.

If I skip three days in a row, the recovery is harder than the original habit. Better to do a short, low-quality session than skip and reset.
